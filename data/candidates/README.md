# Reviewed candidate archive

These records are the reviewed source artifacts for promoted supervised training or validation records. They are not the files consumed by training.

Each archived record retains `status: "draft"` to preserve the original review artifact. The promoted copy is in the selected split's `records.jsonl`, and its manifest records the candidate's SHA-256. Candidate source patterns and filenames must not overlap `data/eval/` or `data/eval_v2/`.
